
## Agentic Statistical Programming with Local Models: A Demo

Local models are increasingly capable and powerful, and offer distinct
advantages including (but not limited to) an increased ability to tinker,
privacy and cost.

However, getting tangible information on how set up a 'backend' and to provide
'frontends' can be frustrating as information is frequently scatter across
multiple source and incomplete.

We intend to provide a _public lecture / talk / demonstration_ of working
with local open weight models such as Qwen under the open source large
language model platform Ollama utilizing frontends such as OpenCode and Pi.
We will detail configurations and use, and demonstrate creation of simple
(short enough) tasks
